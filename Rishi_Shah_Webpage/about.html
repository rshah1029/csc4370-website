Why I Made This

I created this website about PlayStation consoles because gaming has been a big part of my life for as long as I can remember. The PlayStation 3 was the first console I ever owned, and it was where my interest in video games really started. Since then, I have owned the PS4 and PS5 as well, so I got to see how the consoles changed and improved with each new generation. Since I grew up playing on all three, PlayStation felt like an easy topic to choose because it is something I already know a lot about and actually enjoy.

Another reason I chose this topic was because of the memories and friendships I have made through PlayStation. When I was younger, I met some of my closest friends while playing games on the PS3 and PS4. Years later, I still play with many of the same people on the PS5. As everyone has gotten older and busier, getting online and playing together has become one of the ways we stay in touch. Because of that, PlayStation means more to me than just the consoles themselves. It reminds me of the people I met and the experiences I had growing up.

A big reason I wanted to build this website was because of how many experiences I have had with PlayStation over the years. Each console reminds me of a different part of growing up, especially the games I played and the people I played them with. Because I already cared about the topic, I wanted to make a website that showed how much the consoles changed over time and why they were important to me. It made the project feel more personal, and I was more interested in putting time into it because I was creating something about a topic I actually enjoy.
